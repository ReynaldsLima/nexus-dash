D-04 locks exactly **6 (family, weight) pairs**. A weight class is legal only when it sits on an element of the matching family — checking weight classes on their own cannot catch the two most likely violations (600 on Bricolage, 700 on DM Mono), so every finding below must be evaluated against the element's resolved font family:

| Family | Legal weights | Tailwind classes | How to recognise the family |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bricolage Grotesque (default sans) | 400, 500 | `font-normal`, `font-medium` | no family class / no `fontFamily` style |
| Syne | 700, 800 | `font-bold`, `font-extrabold` | `.t-heading`, `.t-display`, `font-heading`, or `style={{ fontFamily: 'var(--font-syne)' }}` |
| DM Mono | 400, 500 | `font-normal`, `font-medium` | `font-mono` or `.t-label` |

**D.1 — zero 600 anywhere.** `font-semibold` must appear **ZERO** times across `$T12_FILES`. There is **no carve-out**.
